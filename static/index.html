    ⓘ

    Important limitation. The model returns a regression score scaled from 0 to 100, not a calibrated probability of layoffs. The risk bands are application-defined, and what-if comparisons are hypothetical model responses—not causal effects. This tool should not be used as the sole basis for employment, financial, or business decisions.
